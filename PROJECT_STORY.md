### Why I Built This Dashboard
One challenge organizations face is having large volumes of sales data but limited visibility into what drives revenue and profitability.
For this project, I approached the dataset from a business perspective rather than simply creating charts.
I designed the dashboard to help decision-makers quickly answer critical questions regarding sales performance, product profitability, customer segments, and regional trends.
Through this project, I practiced turning raw transactional data into a decision-support tool that highlights both opportunities and areas requiring management attention.
This experience improved my understanding of how data analytics contributes to business growth and operational efficiency.
